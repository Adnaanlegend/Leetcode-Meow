LeetThemes + CodeCat

Make every accepted LeetCode submission a little more fun!

LeetThemes + CodeCat is a Chrome extension that adds a playful twist to your LeetCode coding experience.

Every time you successfully solve a problem and receive an Accepted verdict, a cute cat pops up on your screen, meows, and celebrates your achievement with confetti!

Because solving coding problems deserves a little celebration. 🎉

✨ Features

🐱 Cute Cat Companion — A lovely cat appears when you successfully solve a problem.

🔊 Meow Sounds — Celebrate your accepted submissions with an adorable meow.

🎉 Confetti Celebration — Enjoy a burst of colorful poppers after every successful submission.

🚀 Lightweight Experience — Designed to enhance your LeetCode experience without getting in the way of coding.

🎬 How It Works

Open LeetCode and start solving a problem.

Submit your solution.

Once LeetCode confirms your submission as Accepted, CodeCat springs into action.

Your cat appears, meows, and celebrates your success with confetti!

No celebration for wrong answers. Only victories! 😼

📦 Installation (Don't forget to star) 

Install from source

Download this repository:

Open Google Chrome and navigate to:

chrome://extensions

Enable Developer mode.

Click Load unpacked.

Select the extension's directory.

Open LeetCode and start solving problems!

🎯 Motivation

Coding can sometimes feel repetitive, especially when solving problem after problem on LeetCode.

LeetThemes + CodeCat was created to make the journey more enjoyable by adding a small moment of happiness to every successful submission.

It's a reminder that every accepted solution is progress.
